Burger

burger is a popular dish consisting of a cooked, flat, round patty—usually ground beef—placed inside a sliced bun or bread roll. It is commonly served with toppings such as lettuce, tomato, onion, cheese, and condiments like ketchup, mustard, or mayonnaise

Ingredients

  • Beef
  • Brioche
  • Pickles
  • Sauces
  • Extras
  • Potato Rol
  • Seasonings

Procedure

For a single classic burger, you will need (0.25) lb (approx. (115)g) of ground beef and a few simple seasonings.


  1. Season the Meat

    2. Place the 1/4 lb of beef in a small bowl. Add the salt, pepper, and 3/4 tsp of Worcestershire sauce. Mix very gently with your fingers just until combined.

  2. Form the Patty

    3. Roll the meat into a ball and flatten it until it is about 1/2 inch wider than your bun (it will shrink during cooking). Press a small indent into the center with your thumb to keep it flat.

  3. Toast the Bun

    4. Heat a skillet over medium heat. Place your buttered bun face-down for about 45 seconds until golden, then set it aside.

  4. Sear the Patty

    5. Turn the heat to medium-high. Once the pan is hot, add the patty. Cook for 3 to 4 minutes without touching it to get a dark brown crust.

  5. Flip and Melt

    6. Flip the patty. If you want a cheeseburger, place a slice of cheese on top immediately. Cook for another 3 minutes. For a perfect melt, put a lid over the pan for the last \(30\) seconds

  6. Build Your Burger

    7. Stack your ingredients on the bottom bun in this order: sauce, lettuce, tomato, the hot patty with cheese, pickles, and the top bun.

    Final Result:

    To make 1 burger, you need 0.25 lb 4 oz of ground beef, seasoned and cooked for approximately 6-8 minutes total.
